Show on TV (AirPlay & cast)

Browsers can’t stream the whole app through AirPlay like a native video app. Use screen mirroring (or Chrome Cast tab) with TV mode for a big timer.

  • iPhone / iPad: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → pick your Apple TV or AirPlay display.
  • Mac: Control Center → Screen Mirroring, or the AirPlay icon in the menu bar.
  • Android / Chrome: Chrome menu → Cast → Cast tab → Chromecast / Google TV.
  • Samsung / Smart View: Quick panel → Smart View (or your TV’s wireless display option).

Tip: enable TV mode first (huge timer, less chrome), then mirror. Keep this phone unmuted for voice countdowns and beeps.
