About

WorkOut.fit (also branded Plan4Strength) is a browser-based workout planner. Generate a plan for your gear and days, run timed sessions, and save history when signed in. No native app.

Open the Planner to generate a plan — no account needed. Sign in to save plans, track sessions in History, and log body weight.
